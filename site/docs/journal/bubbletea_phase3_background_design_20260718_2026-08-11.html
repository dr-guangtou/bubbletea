date: 2026-08-11 repo: bubbletea branch: phase3_background_design_20260718 tags: - journal - phase3 - background - gaia

Progress

  • Completed verified selector measurements for all 398 large-sky development fields: 8,001,113 Gaia sources, 403,920 passing sources, and 800.213480 square degrees.
  • Compared six frozen fitted models, preserved the extreme sky_field_0317 result, and reassigned the selected fitted model to a secondary diagnostic role.
  • Froze and compared three direct random-field procedures without opening any of the 102 reserved validation fields or making an archive query.
  • Selected the simplest procedure: use all measured development fields in the same broad absolute-Galactic-latitude band and preserve their identities and densities.
  • Measured a typical held-out density difference of a factor of 1.182; the direct 68% and 95% ranges contained 65.6% and 91.0% of development fields.
  • Required future host reports to show both a valid outer-annulus density and the direct random-field distribution, including their numerical difference and ratio.

Lessons Learned

  • Direct measurements are a clearer primary background reference than a fitted curve when the comparison-field densities are already available.
  • An extreme field should remain an explicit out-of-range result instead of being deleted or hidden by a smooth prediction.
  • Automatic repository-wide lint fixes must be scoped carefully because legacy files can be rewritten before pre-existing failures are reported.

Key Issues

  • The 102 reserved fields remain unopened and require one-time evaluation on a new branch without changing the frozen direct procedure.
  • The measured 95% development range achieved 91.0% coverage; the reserved-field evaluation must report whether that shortfall persists without retuning.
  • Real host work still requires calibration of the central exclusion and outer annuli before the official nearby-galaxy run.
  • Repository-wide pre-commit remains blocked by 13 pre-existing Ruff errors in archived notebooks and older scripts; all task-specific hooks pass.

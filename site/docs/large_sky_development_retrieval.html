Large-sky development retrieval benchmark and pilot

Status: Single-field benchmark and 12-field development pilot complete Date: 2026-07-19 Issues: BT-009, BT-022

What was measured

All fields use the frozen 0.8-degree circles, exact spherical areas, 19 raw Gaia columns, G-magnitude range, 5,000-row strip limit, selector version, and probability threshold. Gaia was queried only for the 12 frozen development fields. None of the 102 held-back validation fields was queried or scored.

Raw Gaia rows are stored as verified strip checkpoints outside the repository at ~/Dropbox/work/data/bubbletea/gaia_large_sky_v1. The repository retains measured strip plans, compact field summaries, and provenance manifests.

A passing source is a search candidate selected by point_source_logistic_model_v1; it is not a confirmed UCD.

Single-field performance benchmark

sky_field_0000 is the development field closest to the declared |b| = 20 degree boundary. It was chosen from position alone before its Gaia count was known.

Together, the fields contain 275,404 Gaia sources and 11,583 passing candidates over 24.127040 square degrees. The combined density is 480.083765 per square degree, retained for count and area auditing only. Individual densities span 239.233660–708.748370, with a median of 450.614753.

The pilot used 83 final strips. Planning took 182.383724 seconds in total, retrieval took 109.159825 seconds, and local scoring took 0.256445 seconds during the batch run. The benchmark plan and all 26 benchmark checkpoints were reused without another download. All nine aggregate safeguards pass.

Interpretation and limits

The measured spread confirms that one latitude-only density value will not be adequate. It does not yet show which combination of Galactic latitude, ecliptic latitude, Gaia depth, observation counts, or environment flags best predicts the passing-source density. The pilot was chosen for positional coverage rather than statistical representativeness and is too small for the final model.

The four environment-flagged pilot fields are retained. Their combined result must not be interpreted as an environment effect because the fields were not matched in latitude or Gaia conditions. The full development analysis must measure those conditions and use a declared comparison before deciding whether an environment flag changes the model or sensitivity analysis.

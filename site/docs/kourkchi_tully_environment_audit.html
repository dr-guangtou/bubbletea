Kourkchi-Tully environment audit

Date: 2026-07-18 Catalog: VizieR J/ApJ/843/16 Paper: Kourkchi & Tully 2017, 2017ApJ...843...16K

The raw ReadMe, group table, and member table are stored outside the repository under BUBBLETEA_EXTERNAL_DATA/bubbletea/environment_reference/kourkchi2017. Their SHA-256 digests and source identifiers are recorded in data/literature/sources/kourkchi_tully_2017_groups.json. The external files include the member table, group-property table, association- property table, and CDS ReadMe.

The member table contains 15,004 unique PGC galaxies in 8,826 groups. A nearest spherical audit against all 2,155 current BubbleTea hosts finds 1,794 hosts with a catalog neighbor within 5 arcsec. Two hosts have two catalog galaxies inside that radius and remain manual ambiguities. The other 1,792 unique associations are accepted under accepted_unique_spherical_5arcsec_v1; 361 hosts remain unmatched.

The 5-arcsec identity radius is empirically checked rather than assumed. Eight one-degree shifted control sets, spanning position angles in 45-degree steps, contain 17,240 shifted host positions and produce zero matches within 5 arcsec. The accepted real associations contain no repeated PGC identifier. Ten validation checks pass.

Among the 1,792 accepted associations, the catalog roles are group member, group principal, or isolated principal. These are catalog-derived environment evidence, not permission to assign an inferential background. The two ambiguous and 361 unmatched hosts retain explicit non-accepted states. Catalog distance disagreement is preserved as a diagnostic and does not invalidate an otherwise unique sub-arcsecond identity; the project host distance and the group-catalog distance have different provenance and uses.

Here “principal” means the catalog's main or brightest galaxy for a group. “Isolated principal” means that the catalog treats the object as a one-member group; it does not prove that the galaxy is physically isolated or lacks faint satellites. R2t is the catalog's model-based size estimate for the smaller, collapsed group region. R1t is its model-based size estimate for the larger surrounding association. Neither is a directly measured sharp boundary.

Direct random-field background comparison

Date: 2026-08-11

Contract script: scripts/phase3_background/freeze_large_sky_direct_background_contract.py

Comparison script: scripts/phase3_background/compare_large_sky_direct_backgrounds.py

Input: data/results/background/large_sky_development_scale_summary_v1.csv

Machine-readable result: data/results/background/large_sky_direct_background_comparison_v1.json

Why this replaced a model as the primary reference

The density of Gaia sources passing the frozen selector can be measured directly in random sky fields. A fitted mathematical curve is therefore not required to supply the primary large-sky background reference. The curve remains useful as a secondary check, but it can hide extreme fields behind a smooth prediction.

The direct procedure preserves the actual comparison fields and their measured densities. It reports a median, an arithmetic mean, and central 68% and 95% ranges. These ranges show real field-to-field variation; they are not model confidence intervals.

Procedures compared

The rules were frozen before their performance was measured:

  1. use all training fields in the same broad absolute-Galactic-latitude band;
  2. additionally require the same low, middle, or high third of total Gaia source density; or
  3. use the 20 nearest training fields in Galactic latitude and total Gaia source density.

All fields in the same broad sky block stayed together. A field was therefore tested only against comparison fields outside its held-out block.

Galactic-latitude band 0.1657 0.1671 0.3974 65.6% 91.0% 61
Latitude and Gaia-density third 0.1647 0.1673 0.3790 60.3% 85.9% 20
Twenty nearest fields 0.1647 0.1564 0.3527 61.1% 85.9% 20

The latitude-band procedure is selected by the frozen one-standard-error rule. Its score differs from the best measured score by only 0.0010, while it is simpler, uses roughly three times as many comparison fields, and gives better measured-range coverage. Its overall typical absolute error corresponds to a density factor of 1.182, or about an 18% difference between a held-out field and the comparison-pool median.

The central ranges do not contain exactly 68% and 95% of held-out fields. Their measured coverage is 65.6% and 91.0%, respectively. This shortfall must remain visible and can be checked once on the still-unopened validation fields.

Extreme flagged field

sky_field_0317 has a measured passing-source density of 37,714.863 per square degree. Its 58 same-latitude comparison fields have a median of 390.433 and a central 95% range of 177.324--659.385 per square degree. The field is therefore a factor of 96.597 above the median and far outside the measured range.

This is more informative than forcing a smooth prediction: the direct procedure plainly identifies the field as unlike ordinary random fields in its latitude band. The field remains preserved with its count-blind environment flag. It is not deleted, converted to zero, or allowed to redefine the general background.

Interface with a real host

For each real galaxy, the future result must report both:

  • the density measured in a valid outer annulus around the galaxy; and
  • the median, mean, central ranges, and field count from the direct random-field reference.

It must also report the annulus density minus the random-field median and their ratio. Neither measurement is forced to agree with the other. A missing or invalid outer annulus remains unavailable, not zero.

The random-field reference applies only to |b| >= 20 degrees, the domain actually measured by the frozen grid. The real outer-annulus boundaries and central excluded region still require separate calibration before the official host run.

Status

The synthetic 300-field validation passes all ten safeguards in 0.666 seconds. The 398-field comparison passes all 17 safeguards in 1.405 seconds and makes no archive query. All comparison-field identities and measured densities are retained. The fitted model is now a diagnostic only. No field is approved as a scientific background. At this design-freeze point, all 102 large-sky validation fields were still unopened for a new branch.

The reserved fields were subsequently opened once under the unchanged procedure. The direct validation typical difference is a factor of 1.197, with 63/102 fields inside the development 68% measured ranges and 94/102 inside the 95% ranges. The procedure was not retuned and remains the primary large-sky reference. See docs/large_sky_validation_run.md.

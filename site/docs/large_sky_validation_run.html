One-time large-sky validation run

Date: 2026-08-11

Contract script: scripts/phase3_background/freeze_large_sky_validation_run.py

Retrieval script: scripts/phase3_background/run_large_sky_validation_scale.py

Contract: data/results/background/large_sky_validation_run_contract_v1.json

What is being tested

The 102 reserved fields are fixed sky positions whose Gaia selector results were not used to choose the background procedure. They provide a one-time test of how well that already chosen procedure works on new parts of the sky.

The primary procedure is direct measurement. For each reserved field, it uses the 398 development fields in the same broad absolute-Galactic-latitude band as the comparison sample. The comparison sample supplies its measured median, mean, and central 68% and 95% ranges. It is not a fitted prediction.

The previously fitted sky curve will also be evaluated from the same retrieved measurements, but only as a secondary diagnostic. It will not be refitted, and its performance cannot change the primary procedure.

Rules frozen before the first query

  • The contract contains all 102 field identifiers in a count-blind processing order. Count-blind means the order was computed from identifiers, not Gaia source counts or selector results.
  • Retrieval uses the unchanged 19 selector inputs, frozen selector version, exact circular area, 5,000-row strip limit, 180-second request limit, and 300-second whole-field attempt limit.
  • The default command retrieves zero new fields. Every live run requires an explicit upper limit on the number of fields attempted.
  • Completed fields keep verified raw checkpoints and a receipt outside the repository under BUBBLETEA_EXTERNAL_DATA.
  • A failed or interrupted field keeps a failure receipt and remains missing. It is never recorded as zero sources.
  • Environment flags are reported but cannot remove fields from the primary result.
  • Neither the direct procedure nor the fitted diagnostic may be retuned after a reserved result is opened.

Frozen measurements to report

For both the direct procedure and the fitted diagnostic, report:

  • the typical absolute log-density difference;
  • the root-mean-square log-density difference;
  • the fraction of reserved fields inside the frozen 68% range; and
  • the fraction inside the frozen 95% range.

Also preserve every field-level result, list the ten largest absolute differences, and compare validation performance numerically with development performance.

One-time evaluation result

The table reports both methods and their differences from development. A typical factor is the multiplicative difference corresponding to the median absolute log difference; for example, 1.197 means the measured density typically differs from the reference by a factor of about 1.197 in either direction.

Method Sample Typical factor Root-mean-square log difference Inside 68% range Inside 95% range
Direct latitude-band measurements Development 1.182 0.397446 65.58% 90.95%
Direct latitude-band measurements Validation 1.197 0.299110 61.76% (63/102) 92.16% (94/102)
Frozen fitted diagnostic Development 1.112 0.219669 67.84% 94.97%
Frozen fitted diagnostic Validation 1.128 0.157033 64.71% (66/102) 98.04% (100/102)

During development, each field was tested against a reduced pool because its whole sky block had to be excluded from training. Validation uses all 398 already frozen development measurements because every reserved sky block is separate from them. The resulting validation pools contain 75--85 fields. The comparison therefore tests the final deployed reference rather than recreating the smaller development folds.

For the direct method, validation minus development is +0.013045 in median absolute log difference, -0.098336 in root-mean-square log difference, -3.81 percentage points in 68% coverage, and +1.20 percentage points in 95% coverage. The validation 95% coverage is closer to 95% than the development value, although it remains below 95%; no range was changed after seeing this result.

The eight direct results outside the frozen development 95% ranges are sky_field_0016, sky_field_0215, sky_field_0236, sky_field_0256, sky_field_0269, sky_field_0290, sky_field_0332, and sky_field_0422. They remain ordinary validation outcomes and are not deleted. The two fitted-diagnostic results outside its 95% range are sky_field_0249 and sky_field_0256.

For the fitted diagnostic, the corresponding differences are +0.014158, -0.062636, -3.13 percentage points, and +3.06 percentage points. The fitted diagnostic is numerically tighter on these 102 fields, but it remains secondary. That role was fixed before validation, and the development analysis had already shown that a smooth fitted curve can fail badly for an extreme field.

The count-blind environment flag is descriptive only. Flagged validation fields happen to have smaller direct errors than unflagged fields in this fixed sample; this does not show that an environment intersection improves prediction and is not used to remove or reweight any field.

All 20 evaluation safeguards pass. Every field-level direct comparison pool, measured range, fitted diagnostic value, and residual is preserved in data/results/background/large_sky_validation_reference_v1.csv.

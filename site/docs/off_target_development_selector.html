Frozen-selector results for off-target development fields

Status: Development fields measured; validation selector results remain unopened Date: 2026-07-18 Issues: BT-005, BT-009

What was done

The four frozen development circles were queried for all 19 Gaia columns required by point_source_logistic_model_v1. The selector was then applied locally, using its frozen probability threshold of 0.8277833628791744.

A source that passes the frozen selector has a model probability at or above that threshold. It is a search candidate, not a confirmed UCD. These small circles measure the selector's off-target behavior and cannot establish an astrophysical background by themselves.

Measured development results

Each circle has a 0.1-degree radius and an exact spherical area of 0.031416 square degree. Gaia usable area equals queried area, with no imaging masks.

The four fields contain 413 Gaia sources in total; 44 pass the selector over a combined usable area of 0.125664 square degree. The observed field-to-field difference must be tested explicitly; four fields from only two host systems do not by themselves show that one common density is invalid. No matching tolerance or final background model is selected by this measurement alone.

Validation isolation

The held-back identifiers are 2115:east, 2115:west, 4:east, and 4:west. The script checks these identifiers before every query and again in both output tables. None was queried or summarized.

Pre-selector observing-condition measurements already exist for those sky positions. “Unopened” here specifically means that no frozen-selector probability, decision, or selected-source count has been produced for the held-back fields.

Validation result

All 18 checks pass in 10.560200 seconds. They verify:

  • exactly the four frozen development fields were queried;
  • the four held-back identifiers and their frozen host-role assignments are intact;
  • no held-back field appears in a query or output;
  • measured and retrieved row counts agree;
  • all 19 raw Gaia columns and selector outputs are present;
  • source identifiers are unique and have recorded fingerprints;
  • probabilities are finite and use the frozen selector version;
  • summary counts match the saved source decisions; and
  • Gaia queried and usable areas agree, with zero missing or masked area.

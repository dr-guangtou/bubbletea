date: 2026-08-12 repo: bubbletea branch: phase3_calibrate_host_annuli_20260811 tags: - journal - phase3 - gaia - host_profiles

Progress

  • Froze the first-stage host measurement design: concentric circular rings out to 600 kiloparsecs, a conservative 15-kiloparsec central exclusion, verified Gaia retrieval coverage as the area denominator, and both local and random-field background comparisons.
  • Retrieved and verified the complete NGC 4698 measurement area in six strips: 23,811 Gaia sources over 6.478776 square degrees, of which 2,548 pass the frozen point-source selector; all 14 run checks passed.
  • Produced the radial profile, sky-distribution, selection-parameter, and Gaia flux-excess quality-assurance figures with companion captions.
  • Recorded that the visually reviewed bright example at RA 192.3120615 degrees, declination +8.7036833 degrees is a star despite passing the selector; it has strong nonzero-motion evidence and poor Gaia astrometric fit quality.
  • Defined a bounded follow-up that preserves selector version 1 and measures a separate rule for rejecting sources whose parallax or proper motion is significantly inconsistent with zero.

Lessons Learned

  • A high selector probability does not guarantee that an individual source is an extragalactic point source; measured parallax and proper motion remain important evidence against a UCD interpretation.
  • Gaia's blue-plus-red to broad-band flux ratio is useful quality-assurance context, but the literature reference boundary of 1.7 would not reject the confirmed stellar example and therefore should not be adopted as a BubbleTea cut.
  • Most selected NGC 4698 entries lack the full astrometric measurements needed for a motion test, so missing astrometry must remain explicitly unknown rather than being treated as evidence of zero motion.

Key Issues

  • The 2,548 passing sources are not yet a scientifically clean UCD-candidate list; the frozen selector was designed as a broad first filter and retains at least one visually confirmed star.
  • Any astrometric rejection boundary must be assessed against the full reliable-UCD sample and contaminant samples, not chosen to remove the single reviewed star or to change the NGC 4698 radial profile after seeing it.
  • A future selector version 2 would require a separately specified validation sample and review protocol; this branch does not revise selector version 1.
  • The next task is the bounded development-sample assessment of an astrometric contradiction rule, including the published three-standard-deviation reference.

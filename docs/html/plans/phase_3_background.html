Review

  • The inherited 0.8-degree fields use an obsolete selector and divide by 2.0 square degrees rather than the exact circular area.
  • The inherited Phase IV annulus can be absent under the two-degree cap and does not exclude the host or environment halo.
  • The current host catalog lacks the verified size, halo, and environment fields required to finalize exclusion radii.
  • The query-free fixture evaluates 20 symmetric candidates: 11 accepted and 9 rejected, with a maximum Galactic-latitude mismatch of 7.11e-14 degrees.
  • 1.5 R2t, 2 R2t, and 3 R2t enclose 91.73%, 97.73%, and 99.77% of accepted group members, while one association R1t encloses 86.41% of usable members; no universal safety factor is frozen.
  • The first live-archive test found 121, 100, and 122 Gaia sources in three equal patches at exactly the same Galactic latitude. Gaia coverage and total source density must therefore be measured, but three patches cannot set a tolerance.
  • The five-host review shows larger field-condition variation for the two group cases. Total Gaia density is partly signal-sensitive near a target and cannot be used as a rigid matching requirement.
